Hello again

It’s been a while since my last post, but I’m happy to say that this blog has served its purpose, even if I didn’t end up writing much. Starting this blog was the first of many steps toward becoming a better version of myself.

Over the years, I’ve explored and experimented with many things. Some worked well, while others didn’t. That is fine, the important part is that I kept learning and improving along the way.

One of the biggest lessons I’ve learned is something my university professor often said:

Semplice è bello (Simple is beautiful) - Prof. GF

This idea of simplicity resonated deeply with me. Whether I was writing code, or applying some mental framework to improve my delivery, I noticed that simplicity created better outcomes. Simplicity can be incredibly powerful.

So, my future self, remember: Simple is beautiful. Strive for solutions that are clear, straightforward, and tailored to your needs.
